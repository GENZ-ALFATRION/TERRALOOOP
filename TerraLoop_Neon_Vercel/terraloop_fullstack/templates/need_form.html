GARDENER & FARMER TOOL

Species-based compost planner

Enter the plant or crop species and growing area. TerraLoop will suggest a compost blend and estimate the total quantity using a species-group planning rate.

How estimates work: the planner selects a suggested compost category and an indicative kg/m² rate based on the species group, then multiplies it by the entered area. Tree recommendations are broad area-based estimates and should be adjusted for tree age and canopy size. Always use mature compost; confirm rates with a soil test or local horticulture expert before large applications.
